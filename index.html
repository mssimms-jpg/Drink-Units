    About you

    Your weight and sex make a big difference to how much each drink raises the level.

    ⚠️ A rough guide only – never use it to decide whether you're fit to drive. It assumes you start each drink when you log it and finish it in about 30 minutes for a pint (a bit less for cans and bottles), 20–40 minutes for a glass of wine, an hour for a half bottle and two hours for a bottle – or sooner, if you log your next drink first. It then uses a standard formula (Widmark) with average absorption and an average body clearing about 15mg/100ml an hour – roughly a unit an hour. Food, health, medication, tiredness and plain individual differences can change the real figure a lot, and you can be impaired well under the limit, or still over it the next morning.
